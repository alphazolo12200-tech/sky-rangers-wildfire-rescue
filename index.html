How to Play

🕹 Move: drag anywhere on screen, or use arrow keys.

💧 Scoop water: fly low and slow over the lake until your tank fills.

🔥 Drop water: tap the blue DROP button (or Space) directly over fire.

💨 Watch the wind arrow. Real pilots drop slightly upwind so water lands on target, not past it.

Real technique: Aerial firefighters make several short, low, accurate drops rather than one big pass, this uses less water per unit of fire put out.
